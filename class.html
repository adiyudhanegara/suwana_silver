Silver class

A 3-hour class where you make a silver ring or pendant at the family workbench, and take it home the same day.

What happens in the class

  1. 1

    Choose your design

    Pick a ring or pendant and a motif, or bring your own sketch. We help you keep it simple enough to finish in one session.

  2. 2

    Cut and shape the silver

    You measure, cut and bend 925 silver wire and sheet with hand tools.

  3. 3

    Add the details

    Twist wire for filigree, or set small silver grains, the same way the family does.

  4. 4

    Solder with the torch

    At the charcoal block you join the pieces with a small torch. Someone from the family is next to you the whole time.

  5. 5

    File and polish

    You file the edges and polish the piece until it shines.

    Questions

    Can children join?

    Yes. Children from 7 years old can join the family class, with up to 4 people in total.

    Can I design my own piece?

    Yes. Bring a sketch or a photo, or choose a motif at the bench. We help you adjust it so you can finish it in one class.

    How long until I can take it home?

    The same day. We polish it at the end of the class and you leave wearing it.

    Do I need any experience?

    No. The class is for beginners, and someone from the family guides you the whole time.
